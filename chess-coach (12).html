🎭 Mistake rate
The top suggestion is sometimes swapped for a slightly worse move (never a game-losing one), and you're not told when. Evaluations are hidden while this is on, since they'd give it away. Think for yourself!
🔮 Predict
It learns how that side plays from the moves you make, then guesses the next one. In vs AI it always follows your own side.
